# MyPDF Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

MyPDF is a PDF reader and editor published by MNZ One. This policy applies to MyPDF for Windows (including the Microsoft Store version) and macOS.

## What MyPDF collects

Nothing. MyPDF has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect, store or share personal information.

## Your documents

The PDF files you open, edit, sign or redact stay on your computer. MyPDF works offline and never uploads your documents. Text recognition (OCR) runs locally with the bundled Tesseract engine.

Settings, saved signatures and stamps, and the list of recently opened files are stored only on your computer (in `%APPDATA%\MyPDF` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/MyPDF` on macOS). Uninstalling MyPDF, or deleting that folder, removes them.

## Internet use

- **Microsoft Store version:** MyPDF makes no network requests. The Microsoft Store installs its updates.
- **Version downloaded from GitHub:** once a day (this can be turned off in Settings), MyPDF asks GitHub whether a newer version is available, and it downloads the installer if you choose to update. These requests send only what any web request sends, such as your IP address and the MyPDF version number, and are covered by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children

MyPDF does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
